This repo contains and explains our UMIL@NFFA-DI contributions (by Elena Molteni and Hajar Belgroun) to the NOMAD Yambo parsers for:

1) the correct parsing of atom coordinates and number of atoms by chemical species --> here in the subfolder  /Yambo_atom_coords 

2) the parsing (and plotting) of optical spectra --> here in the subfolder /Yambo_spectra


See also E. Molteni's MDMC master thesis "Extending NOMAD schemas for the UMIL NFFA-DI Theory & Simulation Installation, with a focus on the Yambo code", https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.15880852
